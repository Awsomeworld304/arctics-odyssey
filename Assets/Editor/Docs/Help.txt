[font_size=16][b]Settings[/b][/font_size]
[indent][b]change(key:String, value, value2, save_settings)[/b][/indent]
[indent][font_size=16][color=gray]Available keys: [code]max_framerate (fps), resolution (res)[/code][font_size=16][color=white]
Example 1: [font_size=16][code]Settings.change("fps", 60);[/code][font_size=16]
Example 2: [font_size=16][code]Settings.change("res", resolutions[2]); OR Settings.change("res", 1920, 1080);[/code][font_size=16]
Allows you to change settings from the command line.
